# Fractarium-Web

Website, support and privacy pages for **Fractarium**, a free iPhone app that lets you watch fractals come to
life. Served by GitHub Pages at <https://fractarium.ma-adler.de>.

On the App Store since October 2026: <https://apps.apple.com/app/id6818139386> (App ID `6818139386`). The start
pages link to the store in their language and carry the Smart App Banner tag (`apple-itunes-app`).

| Path | Content |
|---|---|
| `/`, `/de/`, `/nb/` | About the app (English, German, Norwegian) – marketing URL in App Store Connect |
| `/support/`, `/de/support/`, `/nb/support/` | Help & contact with FAQ – support URL, linked from the app |
| `/privacy/`, `/de/datenschutz/`, `/nb/personvern/` | Privacy policy – privacy URL, linked from the app |
| `assets/` | Stylesheet, app icon and Apple’s official “Download on the App Store” badges (unmodified, per language) |

Plain HTML and CSS – no scripts, cookies, trackers or external fonts. The paths are referenced from the
app and from App Store Connect, so they must stay stable.

Contact: fractarium@ma-adler.de
